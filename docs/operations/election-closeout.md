# Election closeout checklist

Use this checklist after an election reaches its formally approved close time.

## Before closing

- [ ] Confirm the election owner has approved the closeout window.
- [ ] Confirm voter access and ballot submission are disabled according to the published rules.
- [ ] Capture the election configuration version and relevant audit-log checkpoint.
- [ ] Confirm outstanding incidents or disputes have an assigned owner.

## Tally and publication

- [ ] Run the approved tally process and preserve its input/output evidence.
- [ ] Have an independent authorized reviewer verify the reported totals.
- [ ] Confirm the published result matches the reviewed tally.
- [ ] Record the publication timestamp and responsible approver.

## After closeout

- [ ] Restrict post-election administrative changes.
- [ ] Preserve required audit and election records under the retention policy.
- [ ] Revoke temporary administrator access.
- [ ] Document exceptions, disputes, and corrective actions.

Never change ballots or results manually to resolve a discrepancy. Escalate to the election owner and preserve evidence.
